ABOUT LIFEFLOW

Making blood donation simple and accessible.

LifeFlow is a web-based Blood Donation Management System designed to connect donors and blood-bank staff in one centralized platform.

From donor registration and eligibility screening to appointments, blood inventory, and blood requests, LifeFlow helps organize the entire donation process.
