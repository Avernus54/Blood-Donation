ABOUT THE SYSTEM

Making Blood Donation Management Simpler

The Blood Donation Management System is a web-based system designed to help manage and organize blood donation activities efficiently.

The system allows staff to manage donor information, record blood donations, monitor blood inventory, and process blood requests in one centralized system.
